About Us

Poll Booth is a secure and transparent online voting system based on blockchain technology. We ensure fair and tamper-proof elections.

Key Features

1

Blockchain Security

All votes are immutably recorded on a decentralized ledger — preventing tampering and providing full auditability.

2

Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Verify vote validity without revealing voter identities with zk-proofs — privacy-first voting at scale.

3

Fast & Verifiable

Quick vote submission with real-time verification and transparent result publication for auditors.

How It Works

1

Register Securely

Voters register using verified credentials; each identity is unique and authorized for the specific election.

2

Verify Anonymously

Smart contracts validate eligibility; zero-knowledge proofs keep personal data private while proving authenticity.

3

Cast Your Vote

Submit your encrypted vote — recorded immutably and available for transparent verification by auditors.
